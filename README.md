# Personal IT security and recovery guidance

A practical, step-by-step guide for advanced everyday users who manage several devices, online accounts, and projects, including for work. It helps you protect what matters, keep a way back when something goes wrong, and keep your protection consistent instead of sporadic. No specialist security knowledge is needed.

**The main ideas**

1. Plenty of knowledge exists about security, backup, and recovery, but most of it is written for companies. This guide adapts it to personal life and personal projects, to keep your digital life and work running.
2. Protection takes effort. Balance it against what you actually need, and choose a level you can keep up.

This guide was made with heavy assistance from AI.

## What it is

- Short sections, each a job you can finish and check: steps, why it matters, tools and examples, how an AI assistant can help, what a good result looks like, and the risk if you skip it.
- Cumulative levels, from scams and a basic inventory (Level 0–1) through backups, lost-device recovery, account compromise, shared access, and delegation. Stop at any level; completed steps stay useful.
- Two optional modules, one for digital assets (wallets, seed phrases) and one for AI agents and their add-ons (skills, MCP servers, plugins), plus worksheets and a dependency-map example.

It reduces risk; it does not remove it. It is about you and your personal setup, not a security plan for a company, and it is not legal, tax, or financial advice.

## AI agent instructions

To work through this guide over several months with your own AI assistant or agent, give it [AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md](AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md) as its instructions. They are not tied to any particular model or vendor. The agent asks which language you prefer and where to keep your notes, plans an even pace, checks your progress and habits, keeps up with updates to this guide, and points you to professional help if something looks like an attack. It never asks for passwords, codes, or keys.
